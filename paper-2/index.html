The two-condition test

Let G be a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff abelian group. The full convex set of normal trace-one states on L²(G) with almost-everywhere nonnegative position–Fourier KD distribution contains an extreme point of infinite operator rank exactly when:

  1. The finite relative indices [K:L] of nested compact open subgroups are unbounded.
  2. Two compact open subgroups are incomparable: neither contains the other.

Either condition on its own is insufficient. In the discrete examples below, the compact open subgroups are precisely the finite subgroups.

ℤ × C₂ × C₂

Incomparable subgroups, but indices at most four. No infinite-rank extreme.

Prüfer group alone

Unbounded indices, but all its finite subgroups lie on one chain. Every extreme has rank one.

Prüfer group × Cq

For distinct primes p and q, the Prüfer p-group supplies unbounded indices and the two coordinates supply incomparable subgroups. Infinite-rank extremes exist.

From Paper 1 to Paper 2

Paper 1 studies a finite-dimensional state space and can give infinitely many different mixed extreme points there. Paper 2 asks whether a single extreme point can itself have infinite rank. Finite quotient geometry is used in the mixed-state result. Infinite rank needs the separate discrete constructions and the transfer to the original group.

Follow the proof in stages

The first two pages give independent obstructions. The third and fourth work together in the converse: construct the witness, then return it to the original group. The mixed-state result draws on the chain argument and finite quotients. The multiplicity result strengthens the discrete construction and lifting, without relying on the mixed-state result.
