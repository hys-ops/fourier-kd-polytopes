Bounded sections force finite rank

An infinite-dimensional ambient Hilbert space is not enough. What if every compact-open quotient has bounded size?

Proof idea

  1. Use reality of the Fourier symbol to restrict which position differences can occur. Block decompositions then force an extreme state into one common position and translation-character sector.
  2. With bounded relative indices, the subgroup generated by all compact opens and their intersection form a largest and smallest compact open. The selected sector has at most that quotient dimension.
  3. Consequently, when the compact opens exist and M is finite, every extreme state has rank at most M. The group may still have infinite-dimensional L².

What it establishes

The finite rank bound rules out one case of the main theorem. If no compact opens exist at all, Spriet’s existence theorem already gives an empty state space.
