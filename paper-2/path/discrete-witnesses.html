Fourier KD positivity · Paper 2 · §3 · The discrete construction

Constructing a genuinely infinite-rank extreme

A finite mixed witness can be transferred to a larger group and still have finite rank. A new construction is needed.

Proof idea

  1. For countable torsion groups, handle the independent-coordinate and Prüfer-type alternatives rather than assuming an infinite product decomposition always exists.
  2. For Cp∞ ⊕ Cp, a mean-vector constraint and a restriction on Fourier support propagate vanishing down the tower. For Cp∞ ⊕ Cq with q ≠ p, alternating null-vector conditions exclude finite-support members of a face and yield an infinite-rank extreme point.
  3. In the mixed-prime construction, the displayed positive trace-class operator witnesses a face from which an extreme point is obtained. Its normalization is not itself asserted to be extreme.

What it establishes

This step supplies an infinite-rank witness inside a suitable discrete group. It is not a limit of finite examples claimed extreme by analogy.
