Returning the witness to the original group

The theorem is about the full state space on the original group, not an auxiliary discrete quotient.

Proof idea

  1. Put N=A∩B for two incomparable compact opens A,B, and let U be the union of the compact opens. If U/N is infinite, it is countable torsion and A/N,B/N remain incomparable. Construct the witness there and use its support sector.
  2. If U/N is finite, U itself is compact. In the torsion subgroup of its discrete dual, annihilators reverse containment faithfully, so the two annihilators remain incomparable; unbounded indices produce arbitrarily large finite subgroups there. Character synthesis exchanges the bases.
  3. The embeddings preserve KD positivity and rank, and ensure that ambient decompositions cannot leave the supporting face. Thus a discrete extreme remains extreme in the full original state space.

What it establishes

This is the converse direction of the main criterion. The Lean theorem includes compatible Fourier realizations for a prescribed position Haar measure.
