Fourier KD positivity · Paper 2 · §5 · Spriet’s pure-hull question

When mixing appears before infinite rank

Having a mixed extreme state is weaker than having one of infinite rank.

Proof idea

  1. If compact opens form a chain, the rank-one result and Spriet’s closed-hull theorem for the extreme set give equality with the trace-norm closed convex hull of pure KD-positive states.
  2. If A and B are incomparable, use the finite quotient (A+B)/(A∩B). Its subgroup structure supplies a finite mixed extreme; lift it to the ambient state space. No unbounded-index assumption is needed.
  3. The manuscript checks why the lifted witness remains outside the trace-norm closed pure hull, not just outside the set of pure states.

What it establishes

This answers the pure-state hull question after Spriet’s equation (12). The question after equation (11), about a closed real linear span, is separate.

What Lean verifies

The displayed “Moreover” equivalence on page 10, §5 is verified in both directions: on a second-countable locally compact Hausdorff abelian group, all Fourier KD-positive states form the trace-norm closed convex hull of the rank-one KD-positive states if and only if the compact open subgroups are totally ordered by inclusion. The case with no compact open subgroup is included.

Lean 4.33.1 checked KDClosure.Hull.pure_hull_classical_traceNorm_iff_chain using the ordinary trace norm, with the final proof independently rechecked by the Lean kernel checker. This verification does not cover the separate assertion about a mixed extreme in a finite-dimensional face and is not human peer review.
