From one infinite-rank extreme to continuum many

Once one infinite-rank extreme exists, how many are there?

Proof idea

  1. In a countable discrete model, the nonzero matrix displacements generate an active subgroup. If that subgroup were finite, extremality would force the range into one finite coset sector, contradicting infinite rank.
  2. Conjugating by a character translates frequency. The stabilizer is the annihilator of the active subgroup, so distinct modulations are indexed by its dual. An infinite compact metrizable group has continuum cardinality.
  3. Injectively lift these states back to the original group. Separability gives the matching upper cardinality bound.

What it establishes

The discrete modulation orbit is Lean-checked in a separate supplement (DiscreteOrbit.orbit_card_of_infinite_active). The full arbitrary-group cardinality statement is a written deduction.
