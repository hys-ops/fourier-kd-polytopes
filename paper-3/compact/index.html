The result

The compact nonabelian classification: infinite rank occurs exactly when the group is infinite.

The Fourier tests range over all central projections of the left regular von Neumann algebra, and the position tests range over all measurable sets. The extreme states considered are normal trace-one states.

How the proof works

The connected proof separates matrix coefficients by their Fourier frequencies. The totally disconnected proof uses finite quotients and two position regions that genuine central tests cannot couple. A compact-kernel lift preserves rank and extremality.

Proof and verification

Written proof. Neither Lean verification nor independent human review is reported. This proof does not cover general noncompact nondiscrete groups.
