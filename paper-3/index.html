Central Fourier KD positivity on nonabelian groups

Separate classifications for compact and countable discrete groups.

Compact groups

The compact nonabelian classification: infinite rank occurs exactly when the group is infinite. Lean-checked instances: SU(2) and PSU(2).

Countable discrete groups

The discrete nonabelian classification: in an infinite group, one nonidentity finite conjugacy class is necessary and sufficient.

Each note uses the center of the left regular group von Neumann algebra as its Fourier measurement. General noncompact nondiscrete groups are outside these results.
