A boundary state with all KD entries positive

Technical ingredient for nonpolyhedrality

The question

To turn separation into a geometric argument, find a point where positivity of the operator is tight but none of the KD inequalities are tight.

How it works

  1. Use the two prime factors to build a CHSH-style reflection operator. Its spectrum allows a scalar shift to a singular positive semidefinite matrix.
  2. The construction bounds its scaled KD symbol strictly enough that all the symbol entries are still positive after the shift. Transfer to the ambient finite group and normalize to trace one.
  3. Small KD-real perturbations preserve strict symbol positivity. Shifting along the identity by the smallest eigenvalue makes each perturbed matrix singular again.

What carries forward

This gives a family of singular matrix directions even though the KD constraints remain inactive. It is used in the next proof and is not something a single pure-hull separator alone supplies.
