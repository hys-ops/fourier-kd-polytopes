An explicit rank-three curve, and how it transfers

Concrete branch; stronger exposure on its stated domain

The question

In the Walsh pair for two qubits, the general phenomenon can be seen directly in a one-parameter family of matrices.

How it works

  1. In the family parameterized by 0 < u < 1, the prescribed kernel vector and six zero KD entries determine a feasible matrix up to trace. The formula has rank three and changes injectively with u.
  2. The sum of nonnegative quantities expressing those kernel and zero-entry conditions vanishes only at the constructed state, giving a functional that exposes it.
  3. Zero extension along the binary-plane subgroup maps the construction into the ambient finite group; the exposed face enforcing that support preserves rank and exposure.

What carries forward

The explicit exposed family applies to groups containing the binary plane. The general mixed interval also covers groups such as C₆ that lack this subgroup; the two claims are different. Both the binary curve and subgroup transfer have Lean declarations.
